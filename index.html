About

Five years of keeping production calm

I'm a Senior DevOps Engineer at Optum Global Solutions, where I've spent the last five years designing, automating, and scaling AWS infrastructure for enterprise clients in insurance and healthcare. My work sits at the intersection of Infrastructure as Code, container orchestration, and CI/CD engineering — turning manual, error-prone releases into repeatable pipelines.

Day to day, that means writing reusable Terraform modules, running Kubernetes clusters built with kubeadm, and leading Jenkins-based deployments across Dev, UAT, and Prod without dropping traffic. When something breaks, I lead the root cause analysis and coordinate across teams until it's actually fixed — not just patched.

I hold a Bachelor's in Computer Science from Palamuru University and I'm currently open to relocation and remote engagements.
